NOTCH+ : READ ME FIRST

Notch+ is free and open source. It is NOT notarized by Apple, because notarizing needs a paid Apple Developer account, and the developer is not able to enroll in it. The app is signed ad-hoc and its source is public, so you can read it or build it yourself.

Because of that, macOS shows a warning the first time you open it. This is normal. To open it once:

  1. Drag Notch+ into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it "could not verify" Notch+. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
     Click "Open Anyway" next to Notch+, enter your password, then click Open.
  You only do this once.

Prefer the Terminal? This does the same thing:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Notch+.app

Then:
  - Open Settings (the gear in the notch, or the menu bar item).
  - Claude Code tab: Connect. You see the exact change first, and a backup is saved.
  - Notifications tab: "Give Full Disk Access..." and drag the Notch+ icon into the list, so Notch+ can read notifications and Messages.
  - Shelf and Basket, General: choose what you want.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.
